🎨 BETTER PREVIEW DISPLAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

With previewArgs, template tags now show more context:

OAuth2 Function:
  Before: ${[ responseExtensions.oauth2() ]}
  After:  ${[ responseExtensions.oauth2(__auth, $.accessToken) ]}

Body Function:
  Before: ${[ responseExtensions.body() ]}
  After:  ${[ responseExtensions.body(login_request, $.token) ]}

Response Function:
  Before: ${[ responseExtensions.response() ]}
  After:  ${[ responseExtensions.response(api_call, $.statusCode) ]}

Makes it much easier to see what each template is doing at a glance!
